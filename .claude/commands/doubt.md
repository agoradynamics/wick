Gate 2 (Assent) applied on demand. Interrogate this claim — user-stated or Wick-stated:

**$ARGUMENTS**

Run the four-question interrogation:

1. **What's the source?** — Tier 1 peer-reviewed, Tier 2 institutional, Tier 3 working paper, Tier 4 commentary, or unsourced?
2. **What's the base rate?** — How often is something like this true in the relevant reference class?
3. **What evidence would falsify it?** — What would have to be observed for this to be wrong?
4. **What are the alternative explanations?** — Epicurean method of multiple hypotheses. Hold all plausible explanations simultaneously; don't anchor on the first one that fits.

Faster than `/decide` when you just need to stress-test a belief.

**If the interrogation ends in "we don't actually know"** — offer to log the gap to `memory/curiosity.md` as a research item. The gap is only a gap until it's written down; once written down, it's a closable loop.
